## Decisions recorded — settled, not open

- **`-` as an opinion placeholder** (6 rows) is folded into `null`.
  Checked case by case rather than assumed: in all six the sentiment is
  carried by a verb, a noun or a comparison, never by an adjective, which
  is the definition of an implicit opinion. See the note in
  `scripts/build_v1_1.py`. Consequence: the null-opinion total differs
  from v1.0, so Paper 3's published null-counts should be reproduced
  against `v1.0-paper3`, which is frozen for exactly that reason.

- **Review `233` is deliberately left unannotated** (annotator's decision,
  12 Aug 2026). It was separated from review 90, whose only row actually
  belonged to review 89. Rather than carry empty rows, its text is preserved
  here so it is not lost if the dataset is extended later:

  > Excellent quality and variety breakfast, large pool with towels provided, excellent Internet connectivity throughout the premises, fruit basket in room, iron, free parking.

- **1,000 vs 1,001.** The published figure of 1,001 is a typographical error.
  Every published percentage resolves exactly at n=1000 and none at n=1001.
